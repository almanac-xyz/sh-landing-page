A NOTE ON HOW THIS WORKS

THE DAY ZERO

The investigation starts the moment Sherlock-Home joins your server — not before.

DAY 0
Sherlock-Home arrives and begins observing. The bot can't see activity that happened before it joined — every case starts clean.
DAY 5
First reliable inactivity data becomes available. This is the minimum window the system needs to start distinguishing quiet members from active ones.
MINIMUM DATA THRESHOLD REACHED
DAY 15
More activity history accumulates. Reports begin reflecting real behavior patterns rather than early noise.
DAY 30
Stronger insights. Enough history has built up to confidently flag long-term inactivity and trends.
DAY 90+
Complete long-term picture. This is where Sherlock-Home is most valuable — full visibility across your community's history.
